FoodDecode

Privacy Policy

The short version: FoodDecode does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Your scan history stays on your device. We don't have accounts, servers, or ads.

1. Who We Are

FoodDecode is a mobile application that allows users to scan food product barcodes and search for products to view ingredient lists, with explanations of what each ingredient does and whether it may be harmful. The app is developed and maintained independently.

For any privacy-related questions, you can contact us at: [fooddecode.app@gmail.com]

2. What Data We Collect

FoodDecode is designed with privacy in mind. We collect no personal information from users.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device identifiers
  • Any health or dietary information
  • Crash reports or analytics
  • Advertising identifiers

3. Data Stored on Your Device

FoodDecode stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Scan history — a list of products you have scanned, including product name, brand, barcode number, date scanned, and ingredient data. This is stored using your device's local storage and never transmitted to any server.
  • Manually entered ingredients — if you manually enter ingredient information for a product not found in our database, this is also stored locally on your device only.

You can delete this data at any time by tapping Clear on the Home screen, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Third-Party Services and Community Contributions

FoodDecode uses external food databases to retrieve product and ingredient information.

USDA FoodData Central

FoodDecode uses the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) FoodData Central API to retrieve food product and ingredient information.

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the barcode number or search term may be sent to the USDA API to retrieve ingredient data. No personal information is sent with these requests.

USDA Privacy Policy: usda.gov/privacy-policy

Open Food Facts

If product information is not available through USDA FoodData Central, FoodDecode may also retrieve product and ingredient data from Open Food Facts, a collaborative open food database.

When using Open Food Facts, only barcode numbers or product search terms are transmitted. No personal information is shared.

Open Food Facts Privacy Policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

User-Submitted Product Reports

FoodDecode allows users to report:

  • Incorrect product listings
  • Missing products
  • Missing or inaccurate ingredient information

At this time, reports are submitted through external Google Forms. Information voluntarily submitted in these forms may include product details and any optional information entered by the user.

These submissions are used solely to improve FoodDecode's product database and ingredient accuracy.

In the future, FoodDecode may migrate these submissions to a secure hosted database platform such as Supabase to better manage community-contributed product information.

We do not sell user-submitted data or use it for advertising purposes.

No Advertising or Analytics

FoodDecode does not use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Behavioral tracking
  • Third-party analytics SDKs
  • Social media tracking tools

5. Camera Permission

FoodDecode requests access to your device's camera solely to scan barcodes on food packaging. Camera data is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. You can deny camera permission and still use the Search feature manually.

6. Children's Privacy

FoodDecode does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation and collects no personal data from any user, making it suitable for general audiences including families.

7. Data Security

Because FoodDecode does not transmit or store personal data on external servers, there is no risk of a data breach exposing your personal information. All locally stored data (scan history) remains on your device and is protected by your device's own security measures.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our end, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. All data stored by the app lives on your device and is fully under your control.

To delete all app data, you can:

  • Tap Clear on the Home screen to delete scan history
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the bottom. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
